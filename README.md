
# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse

## Introduction
**Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse** is an animated superhero movie released in 2018. It tells the story of Miles Morales, a teenager who becomes Spider-Man and discovers that there are other versions of Spider-Man from different dimensions.

## Movie Information
- **Title:** Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse
- **Release Year:** 2018
- **Genre:** Animation, Action, Adventure
- **Director:** Bob Persichetti, Peter Ramsey, and Rodney Rothman
- **Main Character:** Miles Morales

## Main Characters
1. Miles Morales
2. Peter B. Parker
3. Gwen Stacy
4. Spider-Man Noir
5. Spider-Ham
6. Peni Parker

## My Favorite Character
My favorite character is **Miles Morales** because he is brave, determined, and willing to accept his responsibilities even when things become difficult.

## Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because:
- It has amazing animation and visual effects.
- It teaches the importance of responsibility.
- It shows the value of teamwork and courage.
- It has an interesting and inspiring storyline.

> "Anyone can wear the mask."

## My Favorite Lesson
The lesson I learned from this movie is that everyone has their own potential. We should believe in ourselves and never give up on our dreams.
